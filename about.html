About me

I’m Lasse Lakela, a digital services and platform designer with 16 years of experience in digital design, business development and customer experience. My background covers strategic design, brand development, e-commerce and team leadership. In recent years I’ve focused on e-commerce development for large, publicly listed companies.

For me, design is where customer needs, business priorities and technology meet. I start by understanding the wider context, then make the trade-offs visible and turn complex questions into practical decisions.

Beyond individual services, I’m interested in how teams work: how responsibilities are shared, how customer insight shapes decisions, and how shared practices keep design consistent. I’ve built design and technology teams from scratch and helped established ones grow. My approach is analytical and pragmatic, and I help people find a clear direction and follow it through.
